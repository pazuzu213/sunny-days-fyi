Privacy Policy

BrewScan · Last updated September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BrewScan ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our app.

Information we collect

  • Camera images: Photos you capture are sent to OpenAI's API for pod identification. Images are not stored on our servers.
  • Account information: If you create an account, we store your email address and authentication token securely.
  • Saved scans: Scans you choose to save are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase information: Subscription status is managed entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We do not access or store your payment details.

How we use your information

  • To identify Nespresso pods using AI vision
  • To provide personalised brew recommendations
  • To manage your subscription access
  • To improve the accuracy of our pod identification

Third-party services

  • OpenAI: Camera images are processed by OpenAI's GPT-4o vision model. See openai.com/privacy.
  • Apple StoreKit: All subscription purchases are processed by Apple.

Data retention

Camera images are sent to OpenAI for real-time processing and are not retained on our servers. Saved scans remain on your device until you delete them.

Your rights

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Saved scans can be deleted directly from the app.

Children's privacy

BrewScan is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@sunny-days.fyi.
