BrewScan Support

AI-powered Nespresso pod scanner

Support Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Camera not working?

Go to Settings → BrewScan → Camera and make sure access is enabled.

Pod not identified?

  • Make sure there's good lighting — avoid harsh shadows
  • Hold the pod steady in the center of the viewfinder
  • Remove the pod from any packaging or foil
  • Try scanning the colour band or label side of the pod

Subscription & billing

  • Tap Profile → Restore Purchases to recover an existing subscription
  • Manage or cancel via Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • 7-day free trial is available for new subscribers
  • For refunds, contact Apple directly at reportaproblem.apple.com

App crashing or freezing?

  • Force-quit and reopen the app
  • Check for an update in the App Store
  • If the issue persists, email us below
