Privacy Policy

Pawdiac · Last updated September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pawdiac ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information we collect

  • Dog profile data: Your dog's name, birth date, breed, and any traits you enter during onboarding. Stored securely on our servers to power personalised readings.
  • Device notifications: If you enable push notifications, we store a device token to deliver daily reading alerts.
  • Purchase information: Subscription status is managed entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We do not store payment details.

How we use your information

  • To generate personalised daily cosmic readings for your dog
  • To deliver push notifications with daily readings
  • To manage your Pawdiac Pro subscription access
  • To improve reading quality over time

Third-party services

  • OpenAI: Dog profile data is processed by OpenAI to generate cosmic readings. See openai.com/privacy.
  • Apple StoreKit: All subscription purchases are processed by Apple.
  • Apple Push Notification Service: Used to deliver daily reading notifications.

Data retention

Dog profile data is retained for as long as you have an active account. You may delete your data at any time by contacting us.

Children's privacy

Pawdiac is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
