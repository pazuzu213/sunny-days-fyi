Setting up your dog's profile

  • Enter your dog's name, birth date, and breed during onboarding
  • The more accurate the birth date, the more personalised the cosmic profile
  • You can edit your dog's profile anytime from the Profile tab

Daily readings not updating?

  • Readings refresh at midnight in your local timezone
  • Pull down to refresh on the home screen
  • Make sure your device has an internet connection

Notifications not working?

  • Go to Settings → Pawdiac → Notifications and enable them
  • Daily reading notifications are sent once per day in the morning

Subscription & billing

  • Pawdiac Pro unlocks unlimited dog profiles and full cosmic history
  • Manage or cancel via Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • For refunds, contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com
