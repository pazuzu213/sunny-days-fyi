Privacy Policy

Pop Crush · Last updated September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pop Crush ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our app.

Information we collect

  • Account information: If you create an account, we store your email address and authentication token securely.
  • Game progress: Your quiz scores, streaks, completed challenges, and achievements are stored to preserve your progress across devices.
  • Purchase information: Subscription status is managed entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We do not access or store your payment details.
  • Usage data: We may collect anonymised data about which quizzes and game modes you use to improve the app experience.

How we use your information

  • To save and sync your game progress and streak history
  • To manage your subscription access
  • To display leaderboard rankings
  • To personalise quiz recommendations based on your play history
  • To improve game content and difficulty calibration

Third-party services

  • Apple StoreKit: All subscription purchases are processed by Apple.
  • Supabase: We use Supabase to manage user accounts and sync game data securely. Data is stored on servers in the United States. See supabase.com/privacy.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We use Supabase for backend storage, which applies row-level security so that users can only access their own data. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data retention

We retain your account and game data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, all associated data is removed within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Pop Crush is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at support@sunny-days.fyi. You can also delete your account directly from the app settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Your continued use of Pop Crush after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Reach us at support@sunny-days.fyi.
