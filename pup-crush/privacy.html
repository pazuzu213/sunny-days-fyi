Privacy Policy

PupCrush · Last updated September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PupCrush ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our app.

Information we collect

  • Game progress: Your level progress, stars, unlocked breeds, and combo history are stored locally on your device to preserve your game state.
  • Purchase information: Any in-app purchases are managed entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We do not access or store your payment details.
  • Usage data: We may collect anonymised, aggregated data about how levels are played (e.g. completion rates) to improve game balance. This data cannot identify you personally.

How we use your information

  • To save and restore your game progress
  • To manage any in-app purchases or unlocks
  • To improve puzzle difficulty and overall gameplay experience

Third-party services

  • Apple StoreKit: All in-app purchases are processed by Apple. See apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • Apple Game Center (if applicable): Leaderboards and achievements may use Apple Game Center. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Data storage and security

PupCrush stores game progress locally on your device. We do not operate a backend server that stores your personal data. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Children's privacy

PupCrush is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation, and no personal data is transmitted to our servers during gameplay.

Your rights

Since game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing the app. For any questions or concerns, contact us at support@sunny-days.fyi.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through an app update. Continued use of PupCrush after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions? Reach us at support@sunny-days.fyi.
