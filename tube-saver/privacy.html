Privacy Policy

Tube Saver · Last updated October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tube Saver ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Tube Saver app.

Information we collect

  • YouTube URLs: When you paste a link to summarize, that URL is sent to our backend to retrieve the video transcript. We do not store URLs beyond processing.
  • Saved articles: Articles you save are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. We do not upload your library to any server.
  • Clipboard access: The app may read your clipboard on launch to detect YouTube links and prefill the input field. No clipboard content is transmitted.

How we use your information

  • To fetch YouTube video transcripts and generate AI-powered summaries
  • To display article content within the app
  • We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or profiling

Third-party services

  • Anthropic (Claude): Video transcripts are processed by Anthropic's API to generate article summaries. See anthropic.com/privacy.
  • YouTube: Public transcript data is retrieved from YouTube's public transcript endpoints. No authentication is required or stored.

Data storage

Tube Saver does not maintain a user account system. All saved articles are stored locally on your device and are not synced to any cloud service. You can delete all saved articles at any time within the app.

Data retention

We do not retain any user data on our servers. Transcripts and summaries are processed in real time and are not stored after the response is returned to your device.

Children's privacy

Tube Saver is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
