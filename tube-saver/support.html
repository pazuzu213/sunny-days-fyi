Tube Saver Support

Read instead of watch. AI summaries for any YouTube video.

How it works

  • Paste any YouTube URL into the input field
  • Tap Summarize — takes 15–30 seconds
  • Read the AI-generated article with 1-min, 5-min, or full-length views
  • Save articles to your library with the heart button

Supported URL formats

  • youtube.com/watch?v=...
  • youtu.be/...
  • youtube.com/shorts/...

Common issues

  • "No transcript available" — Some videos have transcripts disabled by the creator. Try a different video.
  • Summarizing takes a long time — Long videos (1hr+) take longer. The app allows up to 2 minutes.
  • Article text looks wrong — This can happen on rare videos with auto-generated, low-quality transcripts. The AI does its best but results vary.
